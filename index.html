🛡️ Coping

⚠️

Maladaptive Coping

  1. Disengagement
  2. Avoidance
  3. Emotional suppression
  4. Overworking
  5. Substance use
  6. Self-harm
  7. Isolation
💛

Addressing Maladaptive Coping

  • 💗 Validate the struggle
  • 🌤️ Normalize the reaction
  • 🤲 Collaborative exploration of the maladaptive coping
🌈

Building Adaptive Coping

  • 🔍 Explore previously effective coping — at least 2
  • 💪 Reactivate existing strengths
  • 🌱 Introduce one new emotion-regulation skill
  • 🔁 Review and strengthen coping
🧠

Coping Strategies by Focus

Emotion · Social · Meaning · Problem

💗 Emotion-focused

  • Relaxation technique — DBE 4-4-4-4, progressive muscle relaxation
  • Distraction (sensory) — warm shower, scented soap, calming music
  • Distraction (cognitive) — sudoku, puzzles
  • Distraction (physical) — 10 min walk, tidying part of the house
  • Distraction (social) — casual chat, playing with kids
  • Self-compassion
  • Emotional expression
  • Grounding — 5 see, 4 touch, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste

🤝 Social support-based

  • Talking with loved ones
  • Joining a support group
  • Consulting a mentor / therapist

🌟 Meaning-focused

  • Cognitive reframing — learning experience, focus on personal growth
  • Religion / spiritual coping — prayer, reading spiritual texts

🧩 Problem-focused

  • See the Problem Solving (IDEAL) framework above
